**Second cold review of the seat model, 39e35ab through c997bd4.** A fresh
agent with no context, pointed at `tests/seat-attacks.spec.ts` and the D80
e2e (not `%TEMP%`), asked three questions by name. Every finding was
reproduced here before being relayed. The five repository attack tests pass.
The three answers:

1. **Any path to a seat not through a creator-written row?** Partly. Holding a
   seat always takes some creator's row, but anyone can be the creator of a
   session of their own, and a seat is matched by its bytes (D131). An
   unsigned confirm is a second path (D133). Schema tricks (an author table
   named `_dai_confirm`, `seat=_r_replica`, a view named `_dai_holder`) are
   refused or harmless.
2. **Can a confirmation be forged, replayed, or arrive first?** A signed one
   cannot be forged or replayed: its session is in the signed digest, and a
   copy into another session is refused `BATCH_DIGEST_MISMATCH`. An unsigned
   one can be both (D133). A confirm needs no binding and no minted open seat
   to seat its holder; only the creator can sign one, so that matters only
   through D133.
3. **What does a copy believe offline with only the invite's rows?** It is
   consistent with `IDENTITY-SEAT-ADMITS`. For the joiner who has asked:
   `mySeat` null, `pendingSeat` the open seat, `amCreator` false, and the open
   seat's holder empty. His move for the open seat waits in `moves_pending`,
   and his move for Ada's seat is in `moves_unseated`. For the creator: her
   seat is held and the open seat unheld. This ran the kit's SQL in node, not
   the UI.

**Filed, all rated high, none fixed:**
- **D131:** a seat is its bytes, not its session. A stranger makes a session of
  his own whose creator seat has the bytes of Ada's seat, and his signed move
  lands in her game as White. It lands by file or by mailbox staging, and no
  `SEAT_NOT_HELD` is reported.
- **D132:** any admitted row supersedes another author's row of the same
  entity. `_heads` never asks whether the superseding row's seat may touch
  it, so the seated joiner, or a stranger, deletes Ada's `e4`.
- **D133:** an unsigned `_dai_confirm` under Ada's id seats the forger before
  she confirms, and the kit then never seats the real joiner. This is the known
  step-6 hole, now shown in the seat tables.
