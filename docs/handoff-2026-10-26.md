### 5. 2b2-M4: recovery fetches the head, its parents and its container

- **Built.** A store operation, **Chain**
  (`GET /d/<owner>/<document>/c/<digest>`): the log's entries from an
  envelope back to the root, digest, parents, author and time, no bytes. A
  sealed header that does not carry the container names the envelope that
  does (`containerIn`); a kept record from before it carries the container
  once more. Recovery reads the chain (it must reach a root and name only
  authors enrolled under the owner) and fetches the head, the envelopes it
  names as parents, and the container's envelope, each held to what Chain
  said of it. The head is verified against its parents' checkpoints as
  before; the others on their own signatures, database and document; the
  container's envelope must be on the chain and of the head's release. A
  chain from before `containerIn`, or a store with no Chain (404 `no such
  operation`), is walked up the first parents.
- **Measured**, a notes document kept six times, the local test store:

  | | Envelopes fetched | Bytes |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Before (`d3907784`) | 6 | 2,339,958 (every one kept) |
  | After | 3 | 1,812,976 of 2,340,358 kept |
  | After, the store with no Chain | 3 | 1,812,976 |

  After, the cost no longer grows with the saves: the container's envelope
  and two revisions.
- **Beyond the brief.** The brief asked for the head and the chain's
  metadata. The head's parents are fetched too: the head's checkpoint must
  name its parents' checkpoints, and without them a head whose checkpoint
  names another parent (`restore-verify.spec.ts`, the third test) would be
  restored. **Narrowed, and written in `docs/store.md`:** the checkpoints
  further up are not opened; what they hold is believed of the authors the
  owner enrolled.
- **Owed.** `dai-store-dev` is not redeployed, so it has no Chain, and a
  preview build recovers from it by the walk (three envelopes for a chain
  sealed with `containerIn`). Deploy it, then run the store suite against
