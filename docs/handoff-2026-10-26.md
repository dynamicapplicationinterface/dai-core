- **F.** Before a first change, nothing new. After the first change that
  lands, one line along the bottom edge: "Saved in this browser. Keep your
  work if this browser is cleared. Keep beyond this browser · Not now". It
  takes no focus and moves nothing; typing goes on under it.
- **F.** Once the line is answered, the status in the bar says "Saved in this

  the line says it.

  stays up, and the brief asks that nothing be visible but one quiet line

  the passkey ceremony in that tap. A person who cancels, or has no passkey,
