## Blind Rust level, 5 October

The Rust merge reader (`conformance/readers/rust-merge`) was leveled blind. It
was built only from `docs/format.md`, `conformance/merge/README.md`, the fixtures
and the crate's own source. It now passes 98 of 99 vectors, up from 44. The one
failure is a fixture I believe disagrees with the page (see the end of this
section), so the vector was not changed and the reader was not bent to pass it.

### What changed, and the anchor that required it

- **Session id hashes the roster.** The id is now SHA-256 of the author, the
  seq as eight bytes big-endian, and the canonical CBOR of `[seat, seats,
  close]` as the row holds them, whatever their type. The reader has its own
  canonical CBOR for one column value (#session-id, #session-id-roster,
  #session-id-creator-row, #cbor). This one change accounted for most of the 55
  failures.
- **Declared roster, and max_parties from the manifest.** A creator's seat row
  counts deleted or not. The session is live only when that row is not deleted,
  its roster is valid (16-byte `seat`; `seats` a whole number of 16-byte values,
  distinct, none of them `seat`; one plus their number at most `max_parties`;
  `close` is `any` or `creator`) and its author is no equivocator. `max_parties`
  is read from each copy's `manifest.json` instead of being hard-coded to 2
  (#creator, #roster-declared, #session-void, #fixtures-manifest).
- **Retired rules removed.** Seats minted in seq order (`confirm-minted`), the
  seat-as-row ownership and reseating (`seat-is-row`, `held-row-frozen`), roster
  rows below the creator's seat row counting for nothing
  (`session-from-creator-row`), and `Late` are gone. The version list at the end
  of the page retires each of them.
- **Creator's seat row is immutable.** In `_dai_seat`, only creators' seat rows
  by non-equivocators count and are heads. Every other seat row counts for
  nothing, including later versions of a creator's row (#creator-row-immutable,
  #heads-roster).
- **Confirms count for listed values.** A confirm counts when it is by the
  creator, in her live session, and names a value her row's `seats` holds. It
  counts at any seq, deleted or not, superseded or not, and never for her own
  seat (#confirms).
- **Equivocators hold nothing.** A seat whose counting confirms name an
  equivocator is void. None of an equivocator's rows in an author table is
  admitted or reported (#equivocator-holds-nothing, #void). The `# voided`
  section lists both kinds of void. Only a void with two holders reveals the
  creator, because "a seat void because its holder is an equivocator … accuses
  nobody else" (#equivocated-report). The reader keeps the two sets apart.
- **Close rule read from the row.** Under `close=any`, the creator or any holder
  in the close's session may close; under `close=creator`, only the creator. A
  closer who is an equivocator closes nothing (#close-counts, #close-rule,
  #closed).
- **Close is monotone.** A close that counts, followed by a row of the same
  author in that session at a higher seq (in any table, deletes included), makes
  that author an equivocator. The merge that makes this true reports it under
  the lowest header named by a row of his that the merge took in that session:
  one of his closes there, or a row above the lowest of them. When no such row
  was taken, the report is filed under no id (#close-monotone, #equivocator,
  #close-first, #revealing-close, #equivocated-filed-no-id).
- **Forward parents are malformed.** A parent naming the row's own author at a
  seq at or above the row's own makes the row malformed, both at merge and when
  the copy reads a row it already holds (#parent-forward, #parents-own-malformed).
- **Document mismatch.** When the two copies' `manifest.json` views differ, the
  merge refuses the sibling whole. Nothing is taken, no refused batch is
  reported, and `refused` is `SIGNED_VIEW_MISMATCH`. The reader now checks
  `result.json`'s `refused` field (#document-mismatch).
- **Seat value shape.** Only a 16-byte blob names a seat. A row whose seat column
  holds anything else is `SEAT_NOT_HELD`. So is a value no counting confirm
  names, unless the author waits in it (it is an open seat nobody holds, not
  void, and his current binding names it) or the seat is void
  (#seat-value-shape, #seat-not-held, #waiting).
- **Waiting on a parent.** A session author-table row that names, as a parent,
  an id the copy holds in no table is neither admitted nor reported. The merge
  that releases it reports it as if the merge had taken it (#waiting-on-parent,
  #admitted-parents-held, #seat-not-held). The equivocated-parent rule is
  checked first: such a row "is not admitted, not waiting and not reported"
  (#admitted-parent-equivocated).
- **Admission before any merge.** The reader now checks
  `expected-admitted-a.txt` and `expected-admitted-b.txt` where `result.json`
  says `before: true`, and fails if they are missing (README).

### Silences and ambiguities, and the reading taken

1. **Close-monotone looks circular.** Whether a close counts depends on holding
   a seat, and an equivocator holds none. The page settles it ("Whether its
   author is an equivocator is not read here", #close-counts). Closes are
   evaluated against the header equivocators only, and violators are added in
   one pass. Adding equivocators only shrinks what counts, so no second pass can
   find more.
2. **Where AUTHOR_EQUIVOCATED is filed when reasons disagree.** One merge may
   reveal an author two ways, one with a revealing header and one without. The
   reading: file under the lowest revealing header, and under no id only when
   there is none. The page says "the lowest of that author's revealing headers,
   or no id" but does not rank no id against a header.
3. **"One of his closes there" (#revealing-close)** is read as any `_dai_close`
   row of his in that session, deleted or not. "Above the lowest of them" is
   read as above his lowest close that counts.
4. **"His current binding" (#waiting)** is read as a head of `_dai_binding` by a
   non-equivocator, at an id not equivocated, not deleted, whose `seat` is a
   seat value. The page does not say whether a tombstone binding head still
   asks; the reader treats it as asking for nothing.
5. **Roster-table heads in a session that is not live.** #heads-roster does not
   mention liveness, so `_dai_binding`, `_dai_confirm` and `_dai_close` rows of
   a void session are still heads. In `_dai_seat`, a creator's seat row with an
   invalid roster, or deleted, is still a head (as a tombstone where deleted).
   The `session-roster-invalid-*` vectors agree.
6. **The shape of `holder`.** The page never gives it. It is compared as the hex
   of a blob.
7. **Unseated author table, author not a member.** The page names no report for
   this case, so the row is silent (unchanged).
8. **A row that crosses both a session and a seat** is reported
   `ENTITY_OTHER_SESSION` only. A crossing is decided before seat holding
   (unchanged).
9. **Other-seat crossing compares the raw seat column values**, as
   #admitted-no-other-seat says ("whose seat column differs from its own"). It
   does not first reduce them to seat values.
10. **A document with no `max_parties`** (a plain document) is treated as
    unbounded. No roster rule reads it there.
11. **A refused-whole merge** leaves the local copy exactly as it was: no clock
    bump and no `_dai_replicas` added. This follows from "takes no header and no
    row" read as "touches nothing".

### Fixture that disagrees with the page (reported, not changed)

**`session-parent-forward`, A into B (`expected-ba.txt`).** B holds its own
malformed move e5 (`6fc6…:2`). Its parents name `6fc6…:4`, its own author at a
higher seq. The expected dump keeps the row at `6fc6…:4` (a3, in Bo's own
session) with `_r_superseded` = 1. That is what b.db held before the merge:
a cache written while e5's parents were still read as naming a3.

The page says B "reads its parents as naming nothing" (#parents-own-malformed),
that a reader "MUST check the shape before it reads a row's parents for any
purpose" (#parents-malformed), and that a forward parent is malformed
(#parent-forward). `_r_superseded` is "derived" (#row-superseded). Derived from
parents that name nothing, a3 is not superseded. The same fixture's
`expected-admitted-ba.txt` agrees with this reading: it admits e5 as a head on
B, naming nothing. The Rust reader recomputes the cache from the row set and
writes 0. Everything else in the vector passes, both directions.

One of two things should change. Either the generator re-derives
`_r_superseded` for a held row whose parents became malformed under the forward
rule. Or the page says the display cache is not re-derived for rows a merge
does not touch. In that case it also needs to say which rows a merge does
re-derive, because #merge-signed-outranks needs the parent of the removed row
to be a head again (`merge-seal-outranks-cross-entity`).
