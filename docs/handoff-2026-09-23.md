What is **not** done, and was deliberately left for the next sitting:

- **D80**, the deliberate path — a copy that sets `_r_replica` to somebody

  is `mailbox-link-e2e.spec.ts:1474`, held with `test.fail`.

  notification permission asked mid-seal, taking the share sheet away) and
  **D99** (after a merge from a link, the sample game is on screen and the
  invited one is only in the list — first seen 19 September).
- **`docs/identity.md` is the next sitting's brief, and it is in the repository**
  (added 23 September). It replaces the primitive rather than patching it: a
  person is a keypair made once per device, every write is a signed batch, and

  - **Today's fix is subsumed, not undone.** Its rule 2 keeps the 23 September

  - **D80 closes by construction** under its rule 5: a seat is a signed claim,

  - **Its test list is the work order**, nine tests, each red before any runtime

## How this sitting worked, in case it helps

- A test that measures the *first* observable state is the recurring mistake
  here: the store address was measured on the load that followed the link and
  was right there and wrong on every load after (D56); the icon tests read a
  manifest before the document's own was linked; `runner.spec`'s keep test
  waited for an address to change rather than the load it caused (D93).
- Two fixes this week were reverted or corrected because a rule was applied
  without running everything that presses the surface it touched. `launchAddress`
  is named in three files but pressed by twenty-four specs; selecting by what
  presses a thing, not by its name, is what caught them.
