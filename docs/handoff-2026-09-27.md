## Rulings wanted (proposals only; nothing is ruled)

- **D158.** The finding is that the creator's own rows are trusted to be
  stable, and they are not: every later row she signs about the roster
  counts. A proposal in the shape D152 took: the creator's seat is her
  lowest-seq nonce row in the session, and nothing later of hers changes it;
  a confirm counts only as the lowest-seq confirm per seat *among those
  written before* any other author's admitted row in that seat. The skipped-seq
  variant is the hard one: any author can fill a gap in their own seqs
  later. Whether the merge refuses a row below the author's highest seq
  already held from them (a stream, not a set) is the real question, and it
  reaches every table.
- **D159.** Refuse at merge a row whose `_r_parents` is not a flat JSON array
  of `"<32 hex>:<integer>"` strings. Small, local, no format change.
- **D160.** Two readings: keep a deterministic winner (for example the lower
  content digest) and report the author's equivocation, or treat an
  equivocating creator's confirms in that session as void on every copy that
  sees both. Either needs the merge to keep or remember the loser.
