How the turns work

Try one legal move, then play it. Share sends the board through DAI. When their copy comes back, both boards merge into one game, whichever order the moves arrive in.

If you both move at the same turn on separate copies, nothing is lost: the board shows both moves and asks which one stands.

Names label the players; there are no accounts or identity checks.
