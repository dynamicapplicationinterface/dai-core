How the turns work

Start a game and send one invite. Whoever opens it takes the other seat and names themselves. From then on, each move you play reaches them by itself, sealed so that only the two of you can read it.

You can move only on your own turn. When it comes back to you, the board says so, and if you allowed notifications when you invited, your device tells you even with the app closed.

If an invite is opened on two devices, neither can play until you send a fresh one. If two moves ever land at the same turn, nothing is lost: the board shows both and asks which one stands.

Names label the players; there are no accounts or identity checks.
